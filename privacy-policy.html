Privacy Policy — BD Travel Guide

Last updated: 16 October 2026 · App package: com.bdtravelguide.app

BD Travel Guide ("the app") is a travel guide for Bangladesh. This page explains what data the app uses and why. In short: the app has no accounts, collects no personal data, and keeps everything you save on your own phone.

1. Location

If you allow it, the app reads your device's approximate or precise location while it is open, on these screens: Nearby, ATM, Prayer times and Qibla, Trip planner and Route planner. It is used only on your phone to sort places by distance, to work out prayer times and the Qibla direction, and to measure trip distances. Your location is not sent to us or to anyone else, and the app never reads location in the background. You can say no and pick a district by hand instead.

2. Data kept only on your phone

Favourites, places you mark as visited, your travel diary notes, search history, trip plans and routes, budgets, group expense splits (member names and amounts you enter), packing lists, the last weather forecast and exchange rates, and your dark mode and font size settings are saved on your device only. We cannot see them. They are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings.

3. Online services the app uses

  • Weather: forecasts come from Open-Meteo. The app sends the coordinates of the district you picked, not your own location.
  • Currency rates: downloaded from open.er-api.com. The request contains no personal information.

4. When you choose to open another app

Some buttons open other apps or websites, only when you tap them: Google Maps (directions, nearby mosques or ATMs), Google Calendar (add a trip), bus and train ticket websites, and the share sheet (share a plan, an expense split or your location from the Emergency screen). What you send there is shared only with the app or person you choose, and that service's own privacy policy applies.

5. What we do not do

We do not collect, sell or share personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not use your camera, microphone, contacts or files.

6. Accuracy

Travel information such as fares, timetables, prices and prayer times can change or be approximate. Check important details before you travel.

7. Children

The app is a general travel guide and is not directed at children under 13.

8. Changes

If the app starts using data in a new way, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
